Fraud Detection Guide

Learn how to recognize common online scams and protect yourself.

Phishing Attacks

Fake messages or emails that try to steal your personal and banking information.

QR Code Scams

Fraudulent QR codes that redirect you to fake websites or payment pages.

Suspicious URLs

Links with unusual domain names, spelling mistakes or misleading addresses.

OTP Fraud

Scammers may trick you into sharing OTPs to gain access to your accounts.

Common Warning Signs

Unexpected prizes, urgent requests, threats and requests for confidential details.

About FraudLens

FraudLens is a digital fraud detection system designed to identify suspicious messages, URLs, and screenshots. It helps users recognize potential online threats and stay safe from phishing attacks and digital scams.

Our goal is to make digital interactions safer by providing simple, accessible, and effective fraud detection tools.
